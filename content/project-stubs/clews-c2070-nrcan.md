# CLEWs-C2070 NRCan Grant

Status: Ongoing project

## Public Summary

A collaborative NRCan project rebuilding forest and biomass supply modelling for CLEWs-C2070 using open ws3 and fresh_fibre components.

CLEWs-C2070 focuses on innovative integration of climate, land, and water into energy models for addressing Canada's net-zero commitments.
