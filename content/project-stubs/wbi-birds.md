# WBI harvesting and birds

Status: Ongoing research and software project

## Public Summary

WBI harvesting and birds connects forest-estate harvesting scenarios with habitat-association models for boreal landbird species and guilds. The wbi-birds engine runs an epsilon-constraint tradeoff analysis between even-flow harvesting and multi-species bird abundance.
