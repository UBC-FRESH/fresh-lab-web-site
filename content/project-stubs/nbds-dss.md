# NbDS DSS

Status: Past decision-support modelling project

## Public Summary

The Nature-Based Decarbonization Decision Support System (NbDS DSS) is a notebook-based workflow for carbon management analysis in specific case studies. It evaluates scenarios involving harvested wood products and displacement effects.
