# Yancun (Walter) Yan MSc Thesis

Status: Past project

## Public Summary

A completed MSc thesis on integrated optimization of forest-sector climate change mitigation potential in British Columbia.

Yancun (Walter) Yan's MSc thesis developed an integrated mathematical modelling framework to simulate and optimize climate change mitigation potential in British Columbia's forest sector.
