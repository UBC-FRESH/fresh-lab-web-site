# FRESH-Daugherty dynamic-inconsistency reproduction

Status: Ongoing methodological reproduction

## Public Summary

FRESH-Daugherty is an open, reproducible reproduction of the modelling methods and case study in Daugherty (1991) on dynamic inconsistency in linear-programming forest planning models.
